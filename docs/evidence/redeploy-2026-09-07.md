# The three guards go on chain

**2026-09-07.** The hackathon deadline moved from the 9th to the 12th, and the one decision
that had been settled purely by the clock stopped being settled.

`node scripts/attest.ts` had been reporting **MISMATCH** for two days. Not because the live
address was a stranger's contract, but because `src/` had three guards in it that the deployed
vault did not:

| | what it stops |
|---|---|
| `SizeBelowFilled` | `reduceQuote` marking a partially filled slot below the pairs it holds — 12% off the share price with nothing sent anywhere |
| bounded `setRiskParams` / `setGrid` | `minHalfSpread` at 0, `headroomBps` past half a tier, or a zero tick, each of which bricks quoting by typing |
| `MAX_DEPLOYED_BPS` | a `uint16` book-wide cap, where 65,535 reads as a limit and means 655% of NAV |

All three are governor-or-operator only, and the operator, governor and deployer are one key,
so nothing was at risk that this project does not already control. What was wrong is narrower
and worse: **the README told a reader to run a command, and the command failed.**

**`withdraw(idleAssets())` reverted, and that is worth writing down.**
`ERC4626ExceededMaxWithdraw(owner, 9155779275, 9155779274)` — a ceiling exactly one wei below
idle, because `idleAssets()` and the `maxWithdraw` override round in opposite directions. The
amount to withdraw is `maxWithdraw(owner)`: the contract is willing to state its own ceiling,
and asking it is cheaper than reasoning about the rounding.

## The guards, refused on chain rather than in a test

Simulated against the live address as the governor:

```
setExposureLimits(500e6, 65535)  ->  0x23ad1f23  DeployedBpsTooLarge(65535, 10000)
setGrid(0, 1000)                 ->  0x8ad4cdaf  GridMustBePositive()
setRiskParams(1000, 0)           ->  0x95c9f825  HalfSpreadTooSmall(0, 100)

MAX_DEPLOYED_BPS()  10000
MAX_HEADROOM_BPS()   5000
```

`SizeBelowFilled` needs a partially filled slot to reach, so it is covered by two regression
tests rather than a simulation; it is in the same bytecode the three above came out of, which
`attest` matches byte for byte outside the immutables.

## What did not move

`0xFd9c93581ADD42B9B13ba5550542Fc7315775cD9` — the vault the hackathon was submitted against —
keeps 54.30 tUSDC of escrow on open slots and its 33 STT, deliberately, so its own reactivity
handler can still fire and settle them. It is the fourteenth entry in `scripts/lib/vaults.json`
and `scripts/ledger.ts` walks it with the rest: **224 episodes across 14 vaults, −498.19
realised on 21,931.45 of basis**. It ended at per share **0.962755**.

The live vault reads **1.000000**, which is par on a new ERC-4626 and not a return. That
distinction cost this project a published `+2.37%` once already.
