v1 and v2 are the interesting refusals. Both advertise 97.40 of `previewRedeem`, and
neither can pay it: their `totalAssets` is a position stranded on a build with no exit, not
cash. These older builds do not cap `maxRedeem` by idle — that override is one of the audit
fixes, and it was not deployed anywhere until today — so the vault will quote a number it
cannot honour. Simulating before sending is what turns that into a skipped line instead of
two reverted transactions, and the loop continuing past a refusal is why the 61.80 below
them still landed.

Two entries in that table are recorded here because they are permanent: **20 STT** in the
three pre-`sweepNative` probes at nonces 1, 3 and 5, which have no owner, no governor and
no exit of any kind; and **102.13 tUSDC** in v5, whose `totalSupply` is zero with assets
left, so `previewDeposit(1 tUSDC)` returns 0 shares and no deposit can ever buy a claim on
it.

`cancun`, not `osaka`: the explorer's verifier lists osaka and cannot reproduce a build
made with it, which is what left v7 unverifiable.

Then the capital. The old vault could not be emptied by redeeming — it had a slot open, and
the last-share guard is exactly the thing that refuses that:

```
redeem ALL       reverts
withdraw idle    SIMULATES CLEAN
```

So the idle came out and the open slot stayed where it was:


own handler can still fire at expiry. It is now listed in `scripts/lib/vaults.json`, which
is what puts it in `sweepOld`'s path and in the ledger; `scripts/recover.ts` collects the

## The share price now says 1.000000, and that is not a return

A new ERC-4626 starts at par. The vault it replaced was last read at **0.951368**, and its
worst reading on the 31st was −6.84%. Nothing about the redeploy earned that back; the
denominator was replaced. `scripts/ledger.ts` reads all eleven addresses, so the record
survives the reset: **104 episodes, −200.20 realised on 9,136.25 of basis (−2.19%), 18% of
filled quotes adverse.** The strategy still needs that number under about 9%.

## Two things the model did in forty minutes that deserve a flag

At 09:13Z it priced BTC-0-31AUG26-1200 at **0.674** with σ 64.8%. At 09:52Z it priced the
same window at **0.818** with σ 34.6%. Spot moved 30 dollars in that interval; the
probability moved 14 points, and almost all of it came from σ halving. The estimator is
239 minute bars with λ = 0.94 off a testnet oracle, and λ = 0.94 on M1 bars has a half-life
of about eleven minutes — it is designed to move that fast, and on this feed it does. The
three-tick skew cap is what keeps that from reaching the quote as anything more than a
lean, and this is the argument for keeping the cap small rather than the argument for
trusting the number.
